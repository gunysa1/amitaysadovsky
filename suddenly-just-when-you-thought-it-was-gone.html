Suddenly, just when you thought it was gone

Petach Tikva Museum of Art
July 24, 2025 – November 22, 2025

This multi-layered multimedia installation mirrors the dense strata of memory. Combining sound and stop-motion animation projected onto layered paintings, the work explores foundational experiences etched in childhood on Kibbutz Gesher during the War of Attrition.

The events of October 7, 2023, sparked a "return of the repressed," summoning memories that left their imprint on the tender dawn of the artist's life. The exhibition brings to light the story of the artist's grandmother, Tsili Amitai, a post commander during the War of Independence, weaving personal testimony with archival research to explore the liminal divide between past and present.

Mixed media on canvas and stop-motion animation, four channels, 9 min.
